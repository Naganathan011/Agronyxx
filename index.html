01 How AGRONYXX Thinks

Three steps, executed every 2 seconds by our AI engine

1
📡

Sense

Reads live weather (temp, humidity, rain probability, solar radiation) and soil moisture from the field.

2
🧠

Decide

Calculates crop water demand using FAO-56 (ETo × Kc) and picks the best water source: Rain → River → Well.

3
🚰

Act

Starts the pump, irrigates exactly the right amount, and updates soil moisture — all visible on the farm twin.
